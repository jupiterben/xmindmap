## simple-mind-map

This project uses `simple-mind-map` version `0.14.0-fix.3` as a runtime
dependency. Its rendering, layout, history, keyboard, selection, drag, touch,
and export implementations are used through the TypeScript adapter.

Upstream: https://github.com/wanglin2/mind-map
